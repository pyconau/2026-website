This news post is short and sharp: the deadline to secure a PyCon AU 2026 conference t-shirt has been extended by one week\!

Between now and 9am AEST Tuesday 4 August 2026, you can purchase additional shirts, update your size & cut, and [request your shirt be in purple](/attend/wear-it-purple/).

Don’t delay\! We’re working closely with our suppliers for a tight turnaround, and the order must be shipped promptly after cut-off.

To update your order, visit the link in your order email (look for the subject starting “**\[PyCon AU 2026\] Your order**”). You can also request the order be re-sent via [https://pretix.eu/pyconau/2026/resend/](https://pretix.eu/pyconau/2026/resend/)
