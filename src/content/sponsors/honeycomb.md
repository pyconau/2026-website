Honeycomb is an observability platform built for the pace of modern software change. We help engineering teams understand what's actually happening in production by capturing rich, high cardinality event data instead of pre-aggregated dashboards, so you can ask new questions of your systems in real time, not just the ones you planned for. As AI accelerates how fast code ships and behaves, Honeycomb keeps teams' feedback loops fast enough to keep up, turning debugging from guesswork into a guided investigation.
