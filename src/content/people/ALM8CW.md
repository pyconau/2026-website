I am a Lead Data Scientist at Thoughtworks Singapore, where I build LLM agent systems and RAG applications for public sector clients, along with the evaluation workflows that monitor those systems for goal drift, hallucination, and output quality.

Previously at Grab Singapore, I designed and implemented a multi-agent, LLM-driven backend that translates emails and notifications into ten Southeast Asian languages. Built on LangGraph, those concurrently running agentic translation workflows served more than one million users daily. 

My work at Grab also covered distributed systems, anomaly detection on weekly infrastructure costs, and high-throughput microservices handling over five million orders per day.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) from Georgia Tech, Atlanta, USA where I was also a Research Assistant applying graph neural networks and reinforcement learning to digital circuit analysis, such as predicting influence distribution and Hamming weight.

This talk draws directly from my experience evolving RAG evaluation strategies as retrieval complexity grew in a production application.
